# Jobcraft - Send job (Chrome extension)

A Jobcraft side panel on the right of Chrome. It shows the job you're viewing and sends it to your Jobcraft **Add job** page, filled in. You check it there and click **Analyse job** yourself.

- Stays open while you browse, and updates when you open another job.
- LinkedIn and Naukri work out of the box; on any other careers site it asks once for permission to read that site.
- Highlight the description on any page and press refresh: the highlighted text is used.
- Reads only the job page you're on, and sends it only to your own Jobcraft tab. Nothing is applied for or sent anywhere else.

## Install (Developer mode)
1. Open `chrome://extensions` in Chrome.
2. Turn on **Developer mode** (top right).
3. Click **Load unpacked** and choose this `extension` folder.
4. Pin it: puzzle icon in the toolbar, then the pin next to **Jobcraft - Send job**.
5. Click the Jobcraft icon: the panel opens on the right.

After an update: open `chrome://extensions` and press the reload arrow on the Jobcraft card.

Stay logged in to Jobcraft in the same Chrome profile.
